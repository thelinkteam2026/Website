Welcome to FramelinK !

FramelinK is a project made by a group of teens that share the same passions for gaming.

Who are we ?

In this group we have, for now :

  • Two programmers : one who is also a community manager and the other who focuses on things such as game development. By combining their power of creativity, No problems shall appear for the devlopment of their projects
  • Three Designers : They give life to our universe, by their brilliant minds, they design from the global stories to the tiny little details on a character that appears for one milli second.
  • One beta tester : He's the one, the only ! He sacrifies his time and sanity searching for any piece of incorrection from the projects

The group could evolve in the future, maybe more people with the same or different functions

What do we do ?

We have alot of different projects that mostly comes from our designers. But we also need to focus on basic, main tasks such as publishing online

For the other projects, we have :

  • This website ;)
  • "The Pawnless King", a game we are currently working on

  • "Project Playthrough"
